## Why this is only a PARTIAL PASS
The primary Fig. 3 source itself labels and draws the low-rate components as one grouped family: `Neutron + 8B + accidental + wall` (wall/surface). PandaX Table I, however, assigns distinct normalisation uncertainties to accidental (20%), surface (25%), neutron (50%), and 8B (28%). Those distinct nuisance terms are required for a faithful De-Romeri/PandaX-style profile likelihood.

Therefore the grouped vector histogram cannot be silently decomposed into four independent per-bin templates. The 0073a frozen full-PASS condition is not met. No Asimov substitution, proportional splitting, or post-result model was introduced.
