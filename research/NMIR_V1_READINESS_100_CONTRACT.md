# NMIR v1 — 100% readiness closure contract

Date frozen: 2026-09-08
Scope: current Standard-Model-first NMIR v1 discovery funnel and already-authorized G9 real-source closure work.

## Why this contract exists
`NMIR_READINESS` is an audit estimate of research/funnel maturity, not a probability that a useful mechanism exists, not an engineering TRL, and not an article-acceptance probability. This contract freezes what must be true before the repository may state `NMIR_READINESS: 100%`, so the target cannot be moved after seeing later results.

100% does **not** require every candidate to PASS. A scientifically valid scoped negative, reproducible no-go, or genuinely external-data-limited terminal classification can close a branch. Conversely, a large numerical gain does not count toward 100% if its source/transport/actionability assumptions remain unclassified.

## Frozen v1 100% conditions
All conditions R100-1 through R100-6 are mandatory.

### R100-1 — no unreconciled scientific execution
Every substantive Actions calculation initiated before the 100% declaration must be terminally inspected against its prospective contract. Raw result/artifact identity and hashes must be recorded for every result used scientifically. A green workflow alone is insufficient.

There may be no queued/in-progress result whose outcome could change a v1 scientific classification at the moment 100% is declared.

### R100-2 — G9 CCSN solar-transmission closure
The validated Model-S geometry/column/composition chain (0090f, 0092, 0092b-a1) must receive a terminal classification for the generic 10-kpc CCSN 5–50 MeV branch.

The current 0092b-a2 interaction-envelope authority gap must be resolved in one of exactly two admissible ways:

1. **calculation route:** a prospectively frozen, reproducible all-relevant-channel MeV authority envelope passes; then the already-frozen terminal optical-depth criterion is evaluated and the CCSN branch receives `PASS_G9_CCSN_MEV_SOLAR_TRANSMISSION_THIN`, an escalation/transport classification, or a scientific FAIL as dictated by the frozen threshold; or
2. **external-authority terminal route:** a dedicated immutable authority audit demonstrates that the remaining complete nuclear response cannot presently be bounded reproducibly without target-specific external information/measurements that NMIR does not possess, and records the exact missing information. This may close *v1 research maturity* as externally blocked, but it may not be described as physical transparency or opacity.

A heuristic safety factor, post-result representative metal nucleus, allowed-only response promoted to total response, or DIS extrapolation into MeV does not satisfy R100-2.

### R100-3 — G9 CCSN alignment/actionability closure
The old 0090f `100 m` offset edge must not be treated as a physical alignment limit. The prospectively frozen 0093 CCSN alignment-footprint calculation must first terminate and establish the actual numerically supported `mu>=2` footprint or a scoped numerical/topological failure.

After 0093, exactly one prospective real-source actionability gate must use the resulting *already fixed* footprint to classify, at minimum:
- mapping of source sky-angle to source-plane offset;
- whether the generic future CCSN direction can be known early enough for the relevant observer geometry;
- event/alignment temporal window and the source burst duration;
- observer-position/trajectory requirement at approximately the validated 24-AU focal geometry;
- a source occurrence/direction probability or a rigorous reason that no prospective actionable probability can be assigned.

This gate may PASS, yield a scoped negative, or become genuinely externally blocked. It may not silently turn an after-the-fact detected CCSN direction into a prospectively known source direction.

### R100-4 — gain-composition guard remains closed
Before 100%, every v1 conclusion must preserve the frozen separation:
- focusing/state-control gain;
- microscopic interaction probability;
- detector visibility/amplification;
- irreversible neutrino-supplied deposited energy.

No 0090f/0093 magnification may be multiplied into detector events, material response, interaction gain or power unless each factor has independently passed and is physically compatible with the same source/state/geometry. If this compatibility is not established, the corresponding product remains explicitly unauthorized; that explicit non-authorization is acceptable for v1 closure.

### R100-5 — other v1 frontier classes have terminal scope labels
The already-existing non-G9 frontier classes (including G2, G3, G8 and the BSM external-envelope ledger) do not need artificial PASS results. They must, however, have current explicit F10-style scope classifications with no hidden runnable high-value calculation omitted from `RECOVERY.md` / `NMIR_FUNNEL.md`.

BSM response/enhancement remains outside positive v1 claims while the external-envelope completeness gate is locked/blocked. 100% v1 must not be worded as completeness over all conceivable BSM theories or future experiments.

A branch that requires genuinely new experimental data or a genuinely new physical assumption may remain terminally external-blocked without preventing *v1 funnel maturity*, provided the missing datum/assumption and prohibited inference are explicit.

### R100-6 — repository recovery and reproducibility closure
Immediately before declaring 100%:
- `research/RECOVERY.md` and `research/NMIR_FUNNEL.md` must agree on the latest immutable records, exact terminal classifications, current Actions state and exact scope of remaining external blocks;
- `research/RECOVERY_MANUAL.md` must remain sufficient to resume without chat history;
- all result-dependent code used for final v1 classifications must be committed;
- relevant dedicated tests/CI must be terminally checked;
- there must be no `Exact next action` inside the frozen v1 scope that is executable with already-available authority/data and could materially change a v1 classification.

At 100%, follow-on work may still exist, but it must require at least one of:
- a new scientific assumption;
- new external experimental/observational data;
- an explicitly v2 scope expansion;
- engineering/detector optimization beyond the v1 research classification.

## Readiness transition rule
The repository is currently at `NMIR_READINESS: 98%` under the last reconciled authority state.

This contract does not itself earn readiness credit. A clean 0093 numerical closure may justify at most an intermediate readiness update after its artifact is validated and documentation is reconciled. `100%` is forbidden until R100-1 through R100-6 are all satisfied.

## What 100% will mean
Permitted statement:

> `NMIR_READINESS: 100%` means the frozen NMIR v1 research funnel has no remaining executable, unclassified high-value uncertainty inside its declared scope; surviving effects, scoped negatives and external-data blocks are all reproducibly classified.

Forbidden interpretations:
- “NMIR proved a practical neutrino power device.”
- “NMIR proved all possible neutrino-matter mechanisms.”
- “Every surviving numerical gain is realizable on a named source.”
- “A BLOCKED branch is physically null.”
- “100% readiness is a probability that the model is correct.”
