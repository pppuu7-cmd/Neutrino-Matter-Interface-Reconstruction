## Purpose

The paper is relevant: it is a full 3-D MHD `star-in-a-box` simulation for a red supergiant with parameters chosen to resemble Betelgeuse, using the Pencil Code. It reports nonlinear dynamo saturation and surface fields of order hundreds of gauss in the simulated saturated state.

## Why publication authority is not field-profile authority

The recovered publication establishes that an explicit 3-D MHD Betelgeuse-like model exists. It does **not**, in the authority recovered in this audit, provide a released machine-readable numerical snapshot containing the vector magnetic field on the simulation grid together with sufficient coordinates/metadata to reconstruct an NMIR neutrino ray and derive `B_perp(r)`.

Figures, surface maps, quoted saturation amplitudes, or qualitative descriptions are insufficient. Raster extraction from a figure is prohibited for a terminal NMIR authority lock because it would discard vector orientation and would introduce uncontrolled reconstruction choices.

A pathwise field cannot be generated by silently imposing any of the following:

- `B(r)=constant` using the observed ~1 G longitudinal photospheric scale;
- an arbitrary dipole/radial/power-law extrapolation;
- the Dorch saturated surface amplitude as an observationally calibrated Betelgeuse field;
- a surface unsigned-field map as the transverse field along an interior propagation ray;
- a new Pencil-Code simulation whose initial/boundary conditions are merely inspired by the paper but are not the archived model state used in the publication.

## Observational/model compatibility guard

Direct spectropolarimetric measurements provide boundary evidence for a weak, complex photospheric/atmospheric longitudinal field of order `1 G`. The Dorch nonlinear model reports surface fields of order hundreds of gauss. This is not by itself a contradiction because the quantities, averaging, epochs and model semantics differ, but it forbids treating the Dorch field amplitude as a direct observational reconstruction of Betelgeuse.

A terminal model-based 0103 branch could still use a Dorch-like MHD field **as a conditional theoretical benchmark**, but only after a numerical snapshot or fully reproducible model realization is prospectively frozen, its vector coordinates are defined, and the mapping to the neutrino trajectory is explicit. Such a result would be model-conditional, not an empirical prediction of Betelgeuse's true internal field.
