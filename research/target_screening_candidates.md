# NMIR cross-target Standard-Model capture screening — candidate seed

Status: candidate/provenance seed only; no new target is scientifically ranked yet.

## Primary historical screening authority

John N. Bahcall, *Solar neutrino experiments*, Rev. Mod. Phys. 50, 881 (1978), DOI 10.1103/RevModPhys.50.881, calculated solar-neutrino absorption cross sections for nine proposed targets:

- 7Li
- 37Cl
- 51V
- 55Mn
- 71Ga
- 81Br
- 87Rb
- 115In
- 205Tl

This nine-target set is adopted as the first historically motivated screening pool. Ga and Cl are already quantitatively folded in NMIR; the remaining seven are candidates, not PASSes.

## High-priority candidate

R. S. Raghavan, *Inverse Beta Decay of 115In → 115Sn*: A New Possibility for Detecting Solar Neutrinos from the Proton-Proton Reaction*, Phys. Rev. Lett. 37, 259 (1976), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.37.259, proposed the low-threshold 115In inverse-beta channel to the 614-keV excited state and estimated a total solar-neutrino capture rate of order 750 SNU under the solar/neutrino conventions of that era.

That historical ~750 SNU value is **not** directly comparable to NMIR's oscillated B16 Ga/Cl rates and is not imported as a modern event-rate authority. It only makes 115In a high-priority candidate for matched-convention reconstruction because its event-count scale could exceed Ga substantially.

## Screening requirements before ranking

For each candidate, NMIR must freeze a matched primary nuclear response or measured-ft authority; reconstruct threshold and allowed/excited-state branches; fold the same B16 spectra and component-specific MSW survival probabilities used for Ga/Cl; normalize to pure isotope and, separately where useful, natural abundance; compute both events/s/kg and the strict neutrino-only upper bound `E_dep,nu <= E_nu`; and propagate nuclear-response uncertainty.

Any resonant or narrow-line candidate must additionally pass G8 integrated-strength/linewidth/solar-spectrum-overlap bounds before its peak cross section can enter a ranking.

## Priority order for the next pass

1. 115In — low threshold and historically large estimated capture rate; establish modern matched response first.
2. 7Li — classic solar-neutrino target in the same multi-target Bahcall survey; seek primary response tables or ft/branch data.
3. 51V, 55Mn, 81Br, 87Rb, 205Tl — provenance/reaction-threshold audit, then retain only candidates with sufficient solar-spectrum overlap and authoritative response data.

Negative screening outcomes are results and will be retained; targets are not dropped merely because their rates are small.
