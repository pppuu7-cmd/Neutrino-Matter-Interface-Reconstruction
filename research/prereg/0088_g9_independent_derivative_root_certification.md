## New, prospectively frozen independent derivative route
Let the continuous density between Model-S tabulation radii be the same linear interpolation already encoded by `_interp_density`. For physical impact radius `b=x R_sun`, define the continuous projected mass

`M_cyl(b) = integral_0^R 4*pi*r^2*rho(r)*f_cyl(b,r) dr`,

with the same shell-cylinder fraction `f_cyl=1` for `r<=b` and `1-sqrt(1-b^2/r^2)` for `r>b`.

For `0<x<1`, use the analytically differentiated continuous projection as an **independent root locator**:

`dM_cyl/db = 4*pi*b^2 * integral_0^acos(x) rho(b/cos(theta)) sec(theta)^2 dtheta`,

or equivalently

`dM_cyl/dx = 4*pi*R_sun^3*x^2 * integral_0^acos(x) rho(x/cos(theta)) sec(theta)^2 dtheta`.

For the corresponding continuous focal relation `F ∝ x^2/M_cyl`, the dimensionless derivative sign of the signed map is

`D_c(x;z) = (1/R_sun) dy/dx = 1 + q - x*q*(M'_cyl/M_cyl)`,

where `q=z/F(x)` and `M'_cyl=dM_cyl/dx`.

This derivative formula is derived before any 0088 numerical result. Numerical evaluation must use deterministic adaptive Simpson integration (or an algebraically equivalent deterministic quadrature fixed in code) with absolute/relative tolerances frozen at `1e-11` for the derivative integrals. Repeat with tolerances tightened by 4x as an independent numerical replica.

Because the existing authoritative map uses its already validated trapezoidal projected-mass implementation, `D_c` is **not** permitted to redefine map values. Every candidate turning root located with `D_c` must be independently validated against finite differences of the original signed map.

## Frozen root-isolation and certification algorithm
1. Build the same `combined_scan_grid()` only as a deterministic domain partition, not as result-selected turning nodes.
2. Evaluate `D_c` on the entire partition and bracket every sign-changing root. Refine each bracket by bisection/Brent-equivalent deterministic bracketing to `<=1e-12` in `x`.
3. Repeat the independent derivative calculation with 4x tighter quadrature tolerance. Candidate sign-changing root sets must match one-to-one within `1e-9` in `x`; otherwise classify BLOCKED.
4. Independently evaluate the original-map finite-difference derivative surrogate at step scales `1`, `1/2`, and `1/4`. Around every `D_c` candidate, each finite-difference replica must show the same derivative sign reversal and locate a bracket/refined zero within `1e-8` in `x` of the independent candidate. Failure is BLOCKED, not a reason to move a node.
5. Insert only the domain boundaries, independently certified turning roots, and the generating root. No result-selected extra roots/nodes are allowed.
6. Audit every proposed monotone segment on a **pre-fixed dyadic validation mesh** formed by subdividing each original `combined_scan_grid()` interval intersecting that segment into 8 equal subintervals. At every audit point, the original-map derivative surrogate at step scales `1`, `1/2`, and `1/4` must have a consistent nonzero sign matching the segment orientation. Any unexplained sign reversal, zero, or cross-step sign disagreement is `BLOCKED_G9_ROOT_CERTIFICATION`.
7. Tangent/even-multiplicity stationary points that do not reverse derivative sign do not create an additional monotone branch by themselves. However, if any audit point or independent derivative replica becomes numerically consistent with zero without a stable sign-changing root, classify BLOCKED; do not invent a tangent-root threshold or split after inspecting the result.
8. Only if the partition is certified, solve `y=0`, `y=+r`, `y=-r` once per monotone segment, union accepted `|y|<=r` intervals without overlap, and compute cumulative annular area exactly as in 0077.

## Frozen validation criteria

1. generating-root recovery `<=1e-10` in `x`; all solved roots unique to `1e-9`;
2. independent derivative root set stable under 4x tighter quadrature, one-to-one within `1e-9`;
3. each independent root confirmed by all three original-map derivative step scales within `1e-8` in `x`;
4. every certified segment passes the fixed dyadic sign audit at all three derivative step scales;

6. at `x0=0.024`, global one-ring containment/reproduction for 1, 10, 100 m controls within the already frozen **0.5%** tolerance;
7. repeat the final area calculation with every original `combined_scan_grid()` interval bisected once solely as a validation replica; cumulative areas must agree within **0.5%** for every frozen radius/control distance;
8. nominal vs half-step (`1` vs `1/2`) and nominal vs quarter-step (`1` vs `1/4`) certified-kernel cumulative areas must agree within **0.5%**;
9. no unresolved root/segment may be repaired by changing quadrature tolerance, derivative threshold, adding result-selected nodes, or relaxing any 0.5% criterion.
