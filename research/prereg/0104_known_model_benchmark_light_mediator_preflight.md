## Purpose

Test a generic coherent forward-scattering deformation generated by a new vector-like mediator while keeping its zero-coupling/heavy-mediator limits explicit and preventing unsupported transport claims.

## Frozen mathematical contract

For the preflight only, a new flavor-diagonal forward potential is added to the three-flavor Hamiltonian,

`V_X = g_nu g_f n_f / m_X^2`,

in natural units at zero momentum transfer. The synthetic source fermion density is taken as

`n_f = N_A rho Y_f`.

Number density is converted from `cm^-3` to `eV^3` using the fixed natural-unit conversion in code. The effective mass-squared contribution is

`A_X = 2 E V_X`.

The frozen flavor charge fixture acts only on electron flavor. This is a mathematical representative of the forward-potential sector and is not a complete light-mediator phenomenology when the interaction range becomes comparable to macroscopic source scales.

## Synthetic fixture

The preflight chooses a small nonzero dimensionless product `g_nu g_f` and a finite mediator mass so that the new term is resolvable but numerically stable. The exact fixture values are frozen in code before execution and are not best-fit claims.

## Mandatory preflight gates

1. Effective Hamiltonian Hermiticity error `<=1e-12`.
2. Probability normalization error `<=1e-12`.
3. `g_nu g_f -> 0` recovers benchmark 0100 to `<=1e-12`.
4. Increasing `m_X` at fixed couplings suppresses the deformation with the frozen `1/m_X^2` scaling.
5. The declared nonzero fixture produces a finite nontrivial probability shift.
6. Antineutrino matter-potential sign convention is applied consistently.
7. No opacity, scattering cross section, detector response or G9 geometry claim may be inferred from this coherent preflight.
